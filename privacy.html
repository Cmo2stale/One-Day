Privacy

Your privacy in One Day Daily

What you write here is personal. This page explains, in plain words, where it goes and who can see it.

Your entries stay on your phone

Your journal, check-ins, evening reviews, sobriety date, support people and lists are saved only on this device. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Nobody else can see them, including the person who made this app.

If you turn on AI responses

AI responses are off unless you connect them under Me. When they're on and you save a journal entry or ask for a weekly summary, that text is sent securely to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) so it can write a response.

On the way, it passes through the app's own server. That server doesn't save or record what you wrote. The app's maker can see only how many responses are used, not what they say.

If you leave AI responses off, nothing you write leaves your phone.

Where AI requests are processed

Anthropic is based in the United States, so when AI responses are on, the text you send is processed outside Canada. While it's there, it's subject to US law and could be accessed by courts, law enforcement or national security authorities in that country.

Anthropic doesn't use this text to train its AI models. It keeps it only for a short time, for running the service and checking for misuse, and then deletes it. The details are in Anthropic's privacy policy.

If you'd rather nothing you write leaves Canada, leave AI responses off. Everything else in the app works without them.

Speaking your journal

When you speak an entry instead of typing it, the app uses your phone's built-in speech-to-text. That follows your phone maker's own privacy settings.

Deleting your data

Removing the app from your home screen, or clearing this website's data in your browser settings, erases everything saved on this device. There's no copy anywhere else, so it can't be recovered afterwards.

A support tool, not medical care

One Day Daily is here to support your recovery. It isn't a substitute for a doctor, counsellor or sponsor, and its AI responses aren't written by a licensed professional. In a crisis, call or text 988 any time, or call 911 if you're in danger.
